# Xray observability

[Benchmark dashboard](../README.md)

Log delivery measures fresh stdout records received at one or eight subscribers.
The scorecard uses p50 and p95 from the fixed-load run. The recorded health test
compares one sample or a list of samples from an existing Observatory.

| Coverage | Report |
| --- | --- |
| Integrated log delivery | [Latest log rounds](../xray/integrated-lifecycle-accounts.md#log-delivery) |
| Log readiness, delivery, losses and fan-out diagnostics | [GetLogs](../xray/get-logs.md) |
| Recorded health for one or all outbounds | [Outbound health](../xray/outbound-health.md) |
